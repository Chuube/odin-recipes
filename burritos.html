Description:

These crispy beef and bean burritos pack beef, pinto beans, rice, and melted cheese into a golden, pan-crisped tortilla and are served with a tangy jalapeno cilantro sour cream sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 (15 ounce) can pinto beans, drained
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 2 cups shredded cheese, such as Cheddar or Monterey Jack
  • 6 (10-inch) flour tortillas
  • 3/4 cup salsa
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more as needed
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup cilantro leaves
  • 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 1 tablespoon fresh squeezed lime juice

Burrito time!

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add beef and cook, breaking apart with a spoon until browned and crumbled, about 5 minutes. Season with chili powder, onion powder, cumin, salt, pepper, and garlic powder, and cook for 1 minute. Stir in pinto beans and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in rice and remove from heat.
  2. Add half of the cheese to the bottom third of each tortilla. Divide meat mixture among the tortillas on top of the cheese. Top with remaining cheese and salsa. Roll each tortilla up like a burrito.
  3. Heat oil in a large skillet. Working in batches if necessary, place burritos in the pan, seam side down and cook until browned and crisp about 1 minute. Flip burritos and continue to cook until browned on all sides. Repeat with remaining burritos. Keep warm.
  4. Combine sour cream, cilantro, jalapeno, and lime juice in a food processor or blender. Process until sauce is well blended and smooth. Season to taste with salt.
  5. Serve burritos with sour cream sauce.
